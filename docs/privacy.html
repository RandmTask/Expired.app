Privacy Policy

Expired (com.swiftstudio.Expired) · Last updated 2026-07-27

This is an interim policy while a permanent one is finalized. It accurately describes how Expired currently handles data. If you have questions, contact the developer directly.

What Expired stores, and where

Everything you add to Expired — subscription and document names, costs, renewal dates, categories, notes, and any account details you choose to enter (such as an email, phone number, or reference name for your own use) — is stored on your device and synced only through your own private iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit. Expired's developer cannot see, access, or store this data on any server. It never leaves Apple's infrastructure and your own devices.

Anonymous account identity

Expired creates an anonymous, random identifier for your device (via Supabase) to manage your Pro subscription entitlement and, if you use AI Screenshot Import, to apply fair-use limits. This identifier is not linked to your name, email, or Apple ID, and is not used for advertising or tracking.

AI Screenshot Import (optional feature)

If you use the AI Screenshot Import feature to add a subscription from a screenshot, the image you select is sent securely to Expired's server, which forwards it to a third-party AI provider (such as Google Gemini or DeepSeek, or Apple's on-device Apple Intelligence when available) to extract subscription details. Screenshots are processed to return a result and are not stored or used to train models by Expired. Review the relevant provider's own privacy policy for how they handle data sent to their API.

Purchases

Subscription purchases (Expired Pro) are handled entirely by Apple's App Store and RevenueCat, our subscription management provider. Expired's developer receives only your purchase/ entitlement status (active or not) — never your payment details, which Apple and RevenueCat handle directly.

What Expired does not do

  • No advertising or ad-tracking SDKs.
  • No sale of personal data to third parties.
  • No access to your subscription data by the developer — it stays in your private iCloud.

Your control

You can delete any subscription/document at any time from within the app. Turning off iCloud Sync in Settings stops CloudKit sync; your data then stays local to that device only. You can request account/data deletion questions be answered directly by contacting the developer.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: contact the developer via the App Store listing for Expired, or via TestFlight feedback.
